Summary: Size it up: Pop Culture is an offline-first single-player game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All game progress, daily streaks, and preferences are stored solely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Size it up: Pop Culture does not require account creation, login, or internet access to play. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers), nor do we use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

To make the game work smoothly, the app stores gameplay progress and settings strictly on your device using Android local storage (Jetpack DataStore Preferences):

  • Daily Challenge completion dates, scores, and daily streaks
  • Campaign level unlocks and earned stars
  • Endless Arcade high scores, combo streaks, and career averages
  • App preferences (Metric/Imperial unit selection, haptic feedback toggle, blueprint grid toggle, and onboarding status)

This data never leaves your device unless you have Android's standard system backup enabled in your device settings. You can erase all saved game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions

Size it up: Pop Culture does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or storage.

4. Score Sharing

If you choose to tap the Share Results button, the app uses Android's built-in system share sheet to pass a plain-text summary of your round scores to the app you select. No data is sent anywhere automatically.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app contains no third-party advertising networks, tracking scripts, or behavioral profiling tools.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Size it up: Pop Culture does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if future versions of the app introduce new features (such as cloud sync or leaderboards). Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
